End-to-End Execution

Comprehensive Tensile Capabilities

Our fully vertically-integrated workflow guarantees safety, quality, and architectural fidelity.

01

Parametric 3D Form Finding

Using cutting-edge non-linear computational algorithms (Grasshopper, Rhino, Easy Form) to discover natural minimal surfaces and equal-tension membrane geometries.

  • Curvature anti-clastic equilibrium
  • Tension distribution mapping
02

FEA Structural & Wind Load Analysis

Simulating extreme weather stress, monsoons, and cyclic wind uplift using Eurocode and ASTM tension engineering criteria with certified engineering seals.

  • Non-linear geometric deformation
  • Foundation anchor bolt shear tests
03

CNC Fabric Plotting & HF Welding

Fabrication in our climate-controlled factory floor. Automated vacuum CNC cutting plotters and high-frequency radio welding deliver hermetic, molecularly-bonded seams.

  • Sub-millimeter laser cutting precision
  • 100% waterproof HF lap welds
04

Steel Truss & Cable Fabrication

Precision mild steel and stainless steel Grade 316 rigging hardware, turnbuckles, swaged studs, and epoxy polyurethane hot-dip galvanized coating systems.

  • Hot-dip galvanization per ASTM A123
  • High tensile stainless wire cables
05

Turnkey On-Site Erection & Tensioning

Certified rigging specialists handle hydraulic tensioning jacks to prestress membranes uniformly, eliminating wrinkling or future sag over decades of service.

  • Calibrated hydraulic jack prestress
  • Zero-wrinkle membrane finish
06

Structural Audits & Membrane Care

Annual retensioning audits, hardware inspection, ultrasonic seam integrity checks, and specialized non-abrasive membrane washdowns.

  • Annual cable load tension checks
  • PVDF / PTFE lacquer rejuvenation
