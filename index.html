Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about durability, site survey, and installation.

How do I get a free site survey for my residence or factory?
Simply call or WhatsApp our engineering team at +92 302 4001063 with your site location. In Lahore, Islamabad, and Karachi, our team visits within 24 hours to take exact laser measurements and provide a 3D CAD design proposal free of cost.
What is the difference between standard PVC and German PVDF?
Standard PVC is budget-friendly with a 7 to 10-year lifespan. German PVDF features a specialized fluoropolymer self-cleaning topcoat that repels dust, resists heavy Pakistani UV rays without cracking or yellowing, and provides a guaranteed 15 to 20-year operational life.
Can cantilever parking shades withstand severe storms and monsoon winds?
Yes. Every Muzzayan Tensile cantilever structure is fabricated using high-yield structural steel pipes with reinforced concrete anchor pedestals engineered for sustained winds of up to 140 to 160 km/h.
How long does fabrication and installation take?
Residential car parking sheds take only 5 to 7 working days from order confirmation to final erection. Large industrial steel sheds or commercial sports canopies typically take 2 to 4 weeks depending on square footage.
