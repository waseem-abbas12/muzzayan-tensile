Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to your technical and commercial engineering questions.

What wind velocity can Muzzayan Tensile structures resist?
All our structures undergo computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and non-linear finite element analysis (FEA). Standard car parking shades comfortably withstand sustained winds up to 140 km/h, while stadium and custom projects are rated up to 180–220 km/h.
What is the actual service life and warranty period?
Standard PVDF membranes feature a 10 to 15-year warranty with 20+ years lifespan. Premium PTFE Teflon fiberglass systems feature a 25-year structural warranty with an actual operational lifespan exceeding 35 years.
Are tensile membranes 100% waterproof and heat reflective?
Yes. Architectural PVDF and PTFE fabrics are completely impermeable to water with high-frequency welded seams. They also reflect over 80% of solar radiation, dramatically lowering temperatures underneath.
How quickly can a customized project be fabricated and installed?
Standard car parking bays can be fabricated and installed within 10 to 14 business days. Large custom commercial or stadium canopies typically take 4 to 8 weeks depending on engineering complexity and foundation curing.
